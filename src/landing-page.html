How Import Works

Transforming your spreadsheet to a clean database in 3 steps

1. Upload your File

Drag and drop Excel, CSV, or JSON files. With support for complex templates with merged cells and multiple sheets.

2. Auto-Convert Data

Our smart parser detects headers, skips section labels, validates data types, and filters out junk rows automatically.

3. Work Immediately

Your converted data will appear in a collaborative spreadsheet interface, backed by a real database with instant APIs.
